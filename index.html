Abendpfade Studio is the owner’s local production application for the German storytelling channel Abendpfade.

It prepares original adult stories, narration, illustrations, video packages and publication checks. The application connects to the owner’s selected YouTube channel to upload approved videos, manage titles and thumbnails, and retrieve channel analytics.

This site explains the application and its data practices. The production application runs locally and is not a public service. Visitors cannot connect their accounts here.

Our channel

Visit Abendpfade on YouTube for calm, fictional stories for adults. AI tools assist with narration and illustrations; publication requires editorial and technical review.

Control stays with the owner

Google authorization is restricted to the channel owner. Production access is not an invitation for other people to grant account access. See the privacy notice for access, storage, third parties and revocation.
