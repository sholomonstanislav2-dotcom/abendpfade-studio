Privacy notice

This notice covers the owner’s local Abendpfade Studio application. It does not cover Google, YouTube, narration providers or the hosting provider’s own websites.

Access and purpose

With the owner’s authorization, the application reads the selected YouTube channel identity, video metadata, processing status and analytics, including available views, viewing time, impressions and thumbnail click-through rates. It uploads approved videos and thumbnails and can update titles, descriptions, thumbnails and publication settings.

The requested Google permissions are youtube.force-ssl and yt-analytics.readonly. Google’s first permission includes deletion capability; the current application does not implement video, comment or caption deletion. The application does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or payment information.

Storage

OAuth client credentials and access/refresh tokens are saved in private local files on the owner’s Mac, with file permissions restricted to the local account. Channel identifiers, episode records, production assets and retrieved reports are stored in the local project. These files are not published on this site or embedded in its source.

Sharing and services

Tokens are sent to Google only as necessary to authenticate requests and renew access. Video files, thumbnail images and metadata are sent to YouTube when the owner authorizes upload or publication. Google-derived analytics are not sold, used for advertising, or sent to narration or image-generation providers.

Separate content-generation workflows may send story text or image prompts to their selected provider. They do not require sharing Google OAuth tokens. External providers process their own service data under their own terms.

Retention and removal

Local reports and credentials remain until the owner removes them. Revoke Google access using Google Account connections. Then remove the local YouTube token files if no longer needed. Removing local files does not delete videos already uploaded to YouTube; those are managed in YouTube Studio.

This website

These informational pages have no account form, file-upload form, advertising or application analytics script. The hosting provider may process technical connection information and apply its own access controls. No visitors’ Google account credentials are collected by these pages.

Contact and changes

The application is for its owner’s personal use. The current user-support contact is provided on Google’s consent screen. This notice will be updated if the application’s actual data practices change.
